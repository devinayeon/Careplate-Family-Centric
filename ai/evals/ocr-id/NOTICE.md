# Notice for the Indonesian label images

Two groups of images are used only as test inputs for the scan evaluation (`php tools/ai-dev.php ai ai:eval scan --manifest=ai/evals/ocr-id/manifest.csv`). Machine-readable data is in `manifest.csv` (real photos) and `synthetic/manifest.csv` (generated labels): source URL, licence, author, SHA-256 of the stored file and of the original download.

The USDA FoodData Central data is only the chat food catalog and has nothing to do with these images. The older 8 scan samples in `ai/evals/ocr/` came from FDA and Wikimedia Commons, not from Open Food Facts.

### Packaging copyright

These images show third-party product packaging (brand names, logos, illustrations, barcodes, label layouts). They are used here only as evaluation test inputs.

- The CC BY-SA licence only covers what the contributor can license, which is the photograph. It cannot release the copyright or trademarks in the packaging artwork shown. That is residual risk for any public redistribution.
- Some photos include people (a hand, thumb, a face partly visible in the background of id-05) and a barcode. No personal data was added or extracted.

Before any public redistribution of this repository beyond the competition evaluation, remove these images and replace them with team-owned photos taken with permission, and update `manifest.csv` and the results accordingly.

## Synthetic labels (6, `synthetic/images/`)

Generated by `synthetic/generate.php` with PHP GD. The text is drawn with Arial (`C:\Windows\Fonts\arial.ttf` and `arialbd.ttf`, a system font used at render time; the font files are not part of the repository, only the PNG output is). There is no third-party content in these images; they were created by the Careplate project for this evaluation. Truth is exact by construction because the label text and the manifest row come from the same spec array in the generator. They are clean, flat, high-contrast renders and therefore much easier than photographs: they test Indonesian label semantics (decimal comma, kkal, energy distractor, %AKG, Garam vs Natrium, less-than, kJ, dual columns, household serving), not photographic robustness.
